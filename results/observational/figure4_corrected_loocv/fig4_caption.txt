Supplementary Figure. Corrected observational LOOCV recruitment predictions for 1981–2020 on the log10(recruitment) scale. The black line is observed recruitment and the colored line is the year-specific outer-LOOCV prediction from the saved GAM (upper panel) or RF (lower panel). The darker band is the model-only 95% confidence interval, defined as prediction ± 1.96 × sqrt(model variance). The lighter band is the approximate combined 95% prediction interval, defined by adding model variance and residual data variance. Thin dashed boundaries show the data-only residual variability interval, prediction ± 1.96 × sqrt(residual variance). Residual data variance represents unexplained recruitment variability estimated by inner five-fold CV within each 39-year outer-training fold; it is not recruitment assessment or measurement uncertainty. The intervals are approximate, conditional on the existing outer-fold SVD representation, and are not fully nested or formally calibrated.
