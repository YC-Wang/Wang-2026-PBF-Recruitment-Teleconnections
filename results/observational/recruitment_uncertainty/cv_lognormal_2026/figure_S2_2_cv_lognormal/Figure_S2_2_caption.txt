Appendix Figure S2.2. Annual recruitment uncertainty versus propagated GAM-SVD and RF-SVD prediction variability. The black line is the sample standard deviation (ddof = 1) of 500 independent log₁₀ recruitment realizations generated from mean-preserving lognormal distributions parameterized by ISC (2022) period-average recruitment-estimation CVs: 0.21 for 1981–1993, 0.08 for 1994–2016, and 0.325 for 2017–2020. Blue and red lines are the corresponding ddof = 1 standard deviations of 500 held-out GAM-SVD and RF-SVD LOOCV predictions refitted using those same CV-based realizations. Shading marks the three CV periods. These are variability magnitudes, not fractions of uncertainty transmitted from the held-out year's recruitment, and do not represent complete predictive intervals.
