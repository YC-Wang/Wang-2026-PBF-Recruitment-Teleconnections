Figure R2. Recruitment uncertainty propagated through the observational LOOCV modelling framework for (a) GAM-SVD and (b) RF-SVD. Black lines indicate stock-assessment recruitment estimates, dashed colored lines indicate the original LOOCV predictions, and solid colored lines indicate the median predictions obtained from 500 refitted models using assessment-derived recruitment realizations. Shaded regions represent the 2.5th–97.5th percentile ranges of the refitted predictions, quantifying the sensitivity of predictions to uncertainty in the recruitment estimates used for training. All values are shown on the log10 recruitment scale. These intervals do not represent complete predictive uncertainty or the model/data-error prediction intervals shown in Figure 4.
