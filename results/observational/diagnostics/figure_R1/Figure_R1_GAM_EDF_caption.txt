Figure R1. Effective degrees of freedom (EDF) across the 40 leave-one-year-out GAM fits. (a) EDF for the six SVD-derived smooth terms (PO_PC1, AO_PC1, PO_PC2, AO_PC2, PO_PC3, AO_PC3) by held-out recruitment year. (b) Total model EDF, including the intercept, and residual EDF (= 39 − total model EDF); dashed lines indicate medians. (c) Distributions of term-specific EDF across folds. Each fit used 39 training observations. Term EDF was extracted by summing pyGAM edof_per_coef within each three-coefficient smooth term; the extracted term EDF plus intercept EDF equals the reported total EDF in every fold.
